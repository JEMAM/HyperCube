# Skill: Universal Financial Analyzer (DRE & DFC Multi-Setorial)

Acts as a **Universal Financial Analysis Agent** capable of evaluating, parsing, modeling, and explaining Financial Statements (DRE and DFC) for any company in any economic sector.

## Capabilities

1. **Automatic Sector & Taxonomy Detection**:
   - **Varejo / Comercial**: Receita Líquida, CMV, Lucro Bruto, SG&A (Vendas e G&A), EBITDA, Resultado Financeiro.
   - **Indústria**: Receita Operacional, Custos Industriais, Lucro Bruto, P&D, Depreciação/Amortização.
   - **Serviços / Tecnologia**: MRR, ARR, Custos de Serviços Prestados (CSP), CAC, LTV, Margem de Contribuição.
   - **Financeiro / Bancário**: Margem Financeira, Receita de Crédito, Funding, Provisão de Risco de Crédito (PRC).
   - **Energia / Utilidades**: Receita Tarifária, Custos de Energia, EBITDA Regulatório.

2. **DRE & DFC Reconciled Evaluation**:
   - Reconciles net income from DRE with Cash Flow from Operations (FCO) in DFC.
   - Computes profitability ratios (Gross Margin, EBITDA Margin, Net Margin, ROIC, ROE).
   - Calculates liquidity and coverage ratios (Net Debt / EBITDA, Interest Coverage).

3. **Dynamic Scenario Reasoning ("What-If")**:
   - Explains top-down and bottom-up shock simulations across all DAG financial nodes.
   - Generates executive natural language insights for C-level executives.
